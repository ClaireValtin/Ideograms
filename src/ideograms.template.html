How this was drawn, and its limits

  • Band names, ISCN lengths and G-stain grades come from NCBI's human ideogram data (GRCh38), as packaged in ideogram.js. The R pattern is the reciprocal of that G pattern, which is how ISCN treats R-banded ideograms.
  • Grey steps for intermediate bands are an inversion of NCBI's G-stain grades, not measured RHG intensities. Every G-negative band has the same value in the source, so differences in intensity between R-positive bands (including the strongly staining T-bands) are not shown.
  • Variable heterochromatin (1q12, 9q12, 16q11.2, Yq12, acrocentric p11.2 and p13) and stalks are not inverted. ISCN 2024 (section 2.2.2) describes these regions as variable in size and staining intensity with Q-, G-, R-, C- and T-banding, so they are drawn with a separate hatch rather than a guessed tone.
  • At the 550 level the source file leaves gaps in seven places: 7q11.2, 8q11.2, 11p11.1, 12q24.3, 18p11.3, Xp11.2 and Yq11.2. The sub-bands in each were stretched to fill their parent 400-level band, so their relative sizes there are approximate.
  • In the karyogram, all chromosomes share one scale in ISCN units. In the detail view each chromosome is drawn to a height that leaves room for its labels, so detail views are not to scale with each other.
  • The finest level is the GRCh38 idiogram, labelled ~700. ISCN 2024 (section 2.4) states that the GRCh38 idiograms best fit the ISCN 700-band level. GRCh38 is more detailed than ISCN at 6p24 and 9q34.1, and ISCN 850 is more detailed at 1q32, 2p21, 5q13.2, 6p22.3 and 6q21. It was labelled 850 in earlier versions of this page, following NCBI's file names.
  • Band names in karyotypes. Sub-bands such as 9q34.12 or 6p24.3 exist only in GRCh38. An ISCN karyotype uses the band level actually resolved, e.g. t(9;22)(q34;q11.2). Gene positions on this page use GRCh38 names.
  • ISCN also defines 300- and 850-band idiograms. They are not in this dataset, so they are not drawn. Landmark genes (2 to 6 per chromosome, 74 in all) use Ensembl release 110 GRCh38 gene coordinates, placed by interpolation within their band. They are orientation marks, not precise positions. The IGH mark sits at IGHM, inside the IGH locus.

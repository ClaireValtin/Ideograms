What is modelled, and what is guessed

  • Banding. Starts from the NCBI 850-band map (GRCh38, ISCN units), inverted to the R pattern. Condensation is simulated by smoothing that profile, so at lower band levels neighbouring sub-bands merge the way they do on real slides. This is an approximation of condensation, not a physical model of it.
  • Intensity. Each R-positive band gets a random ±12% intensity change so they are not all identical. Real differences between R-positive bands are not in the source data, so these values are illustrative. Terminal R-positive bands are drawn slightly darker, consistent with R-banded chromosome ends being "almost always positive".
  • Heterochromatin. 1q12, 9q12, 16q11.2, Yq12 and the acrocentric satellites and short arms vary in size between homologues here, as they do in people. I could not find a source describing their RHG staining, so their tone is a setting. The default "Pale" is my inference from heat denaturation affecting AT-rich regions more; treat it as unverified.
  • Acrocentrics. Stalks (p12 of 13, 14, 15, 21 and 22) are drawn thinner and paler, and their length varies between homologues, which is normal. The short arms are drawn at about half their ISCN ideogram length, because ideograms draw them larger than they usually appear on slides. That scaling is my judgement, not a measured value.
  • Geometry. Two sister chromatids joined at a primary constriction, a curved axis, small width irregularities and rounded ends. Twisted or crossing chromatids, splayed chromatid ends, overlapping chromosomes and nuclei in the field are not simulated. Relative lengths follow ISCN units, so chromosome sizes are proportional to each other.
  • Magnification. In the one-chromosome view, small chromosomes are enlarged to fill the field; the caption states the magnification relative to chromosome 1. The karyogram keeps every chromosome at the same scale, aligned on the centromere.
  • Optics. Giemsa colour uses Beer–Lambert absorbance with a purple-leaning spectrum. Grayscale mimics a karyotyping camera. Blur, grain, vignetting and debris are cosmetic.
  • Sources for the R-banding behaviour: Chromosome Banding and Mechanism of Chromosome Aberrations (IntechOpen), Chromosome Banding Pattern (ScienceDirect Topics).
